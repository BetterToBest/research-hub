# Compassionism Framework Simulation

An open, in-browser agent-based model of the [Compassionism](https://bettertobest.github.io/research-hub/) framework. No installation is needed.

**[▶ Open the simulation](https://bettertobest.github.io/compassionism-simulation/)** · [Walk-through video](https://bettertobest.github.io/compassionism-simulation/walkthrough/walkthrough.mp4) · [Replication framework](https://bettertobest.github.io/compassionism-simulation/replication.html) · [Source code](https://github.com/BetterToBest/compassionism-simulation)

This folder used to describe version 3.3. The simulation, its documentation and its version history now live in their own repository, [BetterToBest/compassionism-simulation](https://github.com/BetterToBest/compassionism-simulation), so they are versioned and checked with each release.

## What it does

The model follows the same 500 simulated adults for 20 or 40 years, once with Compassionism and once with no programme, in three environments (Reference, Adverse and a Stress Test). It reports, averaged over 500 paired runs:

- days of basic living each adult's resources cover (the [Basic Living Economic Index](https://bettertobest.github.io/research-hub/basic-living-economic-index.html), BLEI), shown first;
- the share of people living below the cost of living, and how far below;
- the share ending with too little wealth;
- who gains and who loses (participants, non-participants, the poorest and richest thirds);
- hours worked, prices, and the programme's cost and how it is paid;
- public costs of poverty avoided (homelessness, prisons, hospital and psychiatric care).

It models Creative Currency Octaves (Basic Units, conversion of expired BU through the Creative Collectives, octaves), essential-service providers (community-owned PTFs and privately owned ones), Public Trust Housing, and joining or leaving each year. Where the Hub's papers are silent, the page labels the choice as Claude's reading of the design.

## Attribution

The math and code of this simulation were engineered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, from the concepts in Duke Johnson's book *Better To Best* and his related vision for eradicating extreme poverty while enriching cultures and supporting human flourishing. The model has not yet been reviewed by an independent economist; the full code is open for anyone to check, and expert collaborators are welcome.
