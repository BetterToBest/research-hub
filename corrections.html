C08 · θ gating

index.html · research-summary.html

Said: θ is 0 below 55% PTF density and scales to 0.25 at 90%+, with no note on how the simulation computes it.

Now says: the same design, labelled as the framework design; the simulation gates θ on its SZH zone-coherence setting, so realised PTF membership does not change θ. The study harness has an off-by-default switch that gates on realised PTF share, for the restudy.

C09 · γ transition

index.html · research-summary.html

Said: γ ramps from 0.12 at entry to 0.20 at Month 6+.

Now says: the same design, plus: the simulation steps by year and applies 0.20 to CCO participants from enrollment and 0.12 to everyone else.

C11 · EDC profile value

index.html · research-summary.html

Said: US baseline near-poverty EDC 0.625; CCO-PTH target 0.025.

Now says: 0.62 is the BLEI paper's own near-poverty profile ($1,300 rent on $2,083 a month), not a measured US baseline; 0.025 is a design target that the simulation uses as an input, so results that depend on it assume it is met.

C13 · $2.75 per food BU

index.html · research-summary.html

Said: 360 BU × $2.75 = $990 per adult per month.

Now says: $2.75 is an assumed market-equivalent value with no source in the paper and differs from ε_food = 2.64 (which would give $950). The simulation credits $990, so changing it would move shipped BLEI figures; the two values are to be aligned in the paper work.

C23 · Drug policy summary: two figures retired

research-summary.html · drug-policy-reform.html (paper body still to be aligned)

Said: $1.5 trillion in prohibition costs versus $847 billion in potential savings over 25 years.

Now: a cost-benefit framework comparing prohibition with regulatory alternatives, using the paper's own cost estimates.

Why: the paper's cost table sums to $4,616B, and $1.5 trillion matches none of its totals. $847B is one line (health cost reductions) of one scenario; the paper's own net benefit is $3,870B (traditional regulation) and $5,044B (integrated).

C24 · Real estate summary: penetration figure restated

research-summary.html

Said: 15–20% market penetration within a decade, $70,000 average household wealth.

Now: 200,000–400,000 participating households by years 8–12; $70,000 of Acre Equity per household over 20 years, a projection with no published model.

Why: the paper's own rollout table gives 8–12% in years 8–12, and the $70,000 is a 20-year figure, not a 10-year one.

C25 · Development paper: "ROI of $1,847" corrected

research-summary.html · community-monetary-development.html (abstract, meta tags, §4.2, conclusion)

Said: a superior ROI of $1,847 per beneficiary; poverty below 5% within 15 years (the summary).

Now: an illustrative net present value of about $2,046 per beneficiary under assumed benefits; poverty converging to a steady state of about 7–12% under assumed rates (the paper's own proposition, corrected Oct 29).

Why: $1,847 is an NPV, not an ROI, and the paper's formula gives $2,046. The $180 a year growing 10% is an assumption. The paper's proof shows poverty settling at 11.8% (7.4% with PTF), never reaching 5%.

C26 · PTH financing summary: $85,000 retired

research-summary.html · pth-independent-financing.html (paper body still to be aligned)

Said: $85,000 in interest savings; sustainability demonstrated at 30–40 households.

Now: the paper's assumed break-even thresholds for each financing model (30–40 households for pay-in-focused trusts).

Why: at the example's own payments, paying the highest rate first saves about $900 and takes 18.3 years. The 30–40 comes from a table of assumed values.

C27 · BLEI table: entry figure corrected, month 33 qualified

research-summary.html

Said: 71.3 days at entry; 730+ days at month 33.

Now: 70.7 days at entry; about 730 days by month 33 if the household also saves about 10% of its financial bandwidth (illustrative).

Why: the paper's own working gives 70.7 (it printed 71.3). The later months need a saving rate the paper never states.
